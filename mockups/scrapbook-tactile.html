Direction 5 — tactile

Direction 4, made to feel like paper. This one is interactive, because press feel can't be judged from a screenshot.

Try these

  • Switch tabs at the bottom — pages slide like sheets across a desk, the incoming edge casting a shadow on the one below.
  • Press and hold a chip on the sighting page. Stickers flatten onto the page under your finger and pop back on release.
  • Tap a chip to toggle it. Unselected lies flat; selected is stuck on, tilted and lifted.
  • Tap any pin on the map to raise the sheet.
  • Mochi's turf is the shaded blob — it appears at three or more sightings. Gravy only has two, so he still gets a dashed line.

What makes it feel sticky

The press is physically motivated rather than a generic shrink: a sticker sits 2px 3px off the page, so pressing translates it by exactly that and collapses the shadow to zero. Down is 50ms and linear-in; release is 190ms with a slight overshoot, which is the pop.

One honest limitation

iOS Safari has no vibration API, so real haptics are Android-only. On her phone the entire feel has to come from motion — which is why the timings above matter more than they would elsewhere.
